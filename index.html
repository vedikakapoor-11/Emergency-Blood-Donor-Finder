ABOUT THE SYSTEM

Connecting Donors With Those In Need

BloodLink is an Emergency Blood Donor Finder System designed to simplify the process of identifying suitable blood donors based on blood group, location, availability, and donation history.

Quick Donor Discovery

Search for suitable donors using blood group, location and availability.

Eligibility Awareness

Donor information and donation history can support appropriate donor selection.

Centralized Records

Manage donors, requests, hospitals, blood banks and inventory in one system.
